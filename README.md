# Dark Science Division

Independent research and engineering by **Samuel Lawson**. Local-first, deterministic, governed AI systems.

## Research

- [trajectory-constrained-adversarial-intelligence-nodes](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/trajectory-constrained-adversarial-intelligence-nodes) — TCAIN: working thesis on adversarial intelligence nodes operating under trajectory constraints.
- [adversarial-trajectory-graphs](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/adversarial-trajectory-graphs) — ATG: working thesis on graph-structured adversarial trajectory analysis.
- [cognitive-resource-allocation](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/cognitive-resource-allocation) — CRA: working thesis on resource allocation across bounded cognitive workloads.
- [governed-concurrence](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/governed-concurrence) — position paper on governed multi-model concurrence.
- [emergent-ai-human-cooperation-thesis](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/emergent-ai-human-cooperation-thesis) — thesis on emergent AI-human cooperation through adversarial optimization (CC BY).
- [covenant-equilibrium](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/covenant-equilibrium) — research paper on prompt-conditioned cooperative reasoning under explicit self-interest constraints.

## Systems

**PRAXIS** — probabilistic risk analysis (Bayesian updating, fault trees, Monte Carlo, cascade/CCF):
[praxis-titan](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/praxis-titan) (public architecture and worked examples), [praxis-engine](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/praxis-engine) (Python runner shell; commercial core), [praxis-mantis](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/praxis-mantis) (architecture/spec release; private core).

**NEO-LAB** — deterministic orchestration of role-separated local LLMs:
[Neo-V3](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/Neo-V3) (current runtime), [ollama-dialog-broker](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/ollama-dialog-broker) (multi-model dialog broker), [URI](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/URI) (local web UI for RRR queries), [RRR_DUAL_CYCLE](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/RRR_DUAL_CYCLE) (adversarial dialogue framework).

Superseded repositories are archived and marked as such; the index in [darksciencedivision-meta](https://github.com/darksciencedivision-ctrl/darksciencedivision-meta) tracks status for every public repo.
